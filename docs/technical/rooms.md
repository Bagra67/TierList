# Rooms

English | [Français](rooms.fr.md)

How rooms are created, read and configured through the API: data model, code and link, settings, endpoints, the lobby, and where it lives in the code. **Built in #83** (API), **#84** (screens) and **#86** (joining with an account) and **#87** (guests, §2.4). What a room is for the user: [user stories](../product/milestone-1/user-stories.md) US-2.1 and US-2.2, [lifecycles](../product/milestone-1/lifecycles.md).

## 1. Functional overview

A room is where a group plays, reached by its **code** or its **link**. It is created from one of the user's templates, who becomes its **room admin**. Its **settings** apply to the next game.

| Action               | Endpoint                                          | Result                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create a room        | `POST /rooms`                                     | An open room on one of my templates with at least one tile; I am its room admin and its first participant. |
| Open a room          | `GET /rooms/{code}`                               | The room, its settings and its participants, for a participant.                                            |
| Join a room          | `POST /rooms/{code}/join`                         | A player seat for me, with my display name; my seat back if I already have one.                            |
| Join as a guest      | `POST /rooms/{code}/guests`                       | A guest seat with a nickname, a browser cookie and my personal link.                                       |
| Come back as a guest | `POST /rooms/{code}/guest-session`                | My guest seat back from the personal link, on any device.                                                  |
| New personal link    | `POST /rooms/{code}/guest-link`                   | A new personal link for the guest recognised by the cookie; the old one stops working.                     |
| Change the settings  | `PATCH /rooms/{code}/settings`                    | The new settings (room admin only); during a game, only late arrivals.                                     |
| Remove a participant | `DELETE /rooms/{code}/participants/{id}`          | Their seat deleted (room admin only, not their own seat), at any time; they can join again.                |
| Ban a participant    | `DELETE /rooms/{code}/participants/{id}?ban=true` | Removed, and their account (or, for a guest, their browser) can no longer join.                            |
| Unban                | `DELETE /rooms/{code}/bans/{id}`                  | The person can join again (room admin only).                                                               |

### Rules

- **Template**: one of mine, not deleted, with **at least one tile** (US-2.1). Otherwise `404` `template_not_found` or `409` `template_has_no_tiles`. Several rooms can use the same template.
- **Code**: 6 characters among `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no `0`, `O`, `1`, `I`, easily confused when read or dictated), random (`secrets`), unique. It is read case-insensitively: `k7p2qx` opens `K7P2QX`.
- **Link**: `FRONTEND_BASE_URL` + `/r/` + the code, e.g. `http://localhost:5173/r/K7P2QX`. The page itself comes with #84.
- **Room admin**: the creator. They get a **participant** row at once: they count in the participant limit even when they do not play (US-2.6).
- **Visibility**: a room is only visible to its **participants**. Anyone else gets `404` `room_not_found`, exactly like an unknown code: the API never reveals that a room exists.
- **Joining with an account** (US-2.4): the code is enough, whoever has it can join. `POST /rooms/{code}/join` gives the user a **player** seat, shown with their display name, without any extra step. Joining again gives **the same seat** back (one seat per user and room), the room admin included. An unknown code answers `404` `room_not_found`. The room row is locked while joining, so two simultaneous joins of the same user make one seat; the unique `(room_id, user_id)` constraint stays the last safety net.
- **Capacity** (US-2.6): at most `FREE_PLAN_MAX_PARTICIPANTS` (10) seats per room, the room admin included even when they do not play. It is a plan limit, a constant like `FREE_PLAN_MAX_TILES`, not a setting. Expired guest seats do not count. A newcomer beyond the limit gets `409` `room_full` with the `max_participants` param.
- **Arrivals lock** (US-2.10): during a game (status `playing`), when `late_arrivals_allowed` is off, a newcomer gets `409` `room_locked`. Outside a game the setting blocks nothing. Whoever already has a seat always comes back (an account through `POST /join`, a guest through the cookie or the personal link), even when the room is full or locked; a removed participant is a newcomer. Checks of `join` and `join_as_guest`, under the room lock: ban, existing seat, lock, capacity (`_ensure_can_arrive`).
- **Settings** (US-2.2), with their default values:

  | Setting                 | Values                             | Default                         |
  | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
  | `item_order`            | `template`, `random`               | `template`                      |
  | `auto_advance`          | `none`, `timer`, `everyone_placed` | `none` (manual only)            |
  | `timer_seconds`         | 5 to 300 s                         | `ROOM_DEFAULT_TIMER_SECONDS`    |
  | `cooldown_seconds`      | 10 to 600 s                        | `ROOM_DEFAULT_COOLDOWN_SECONDS` |
  | `admin_plays`           | `true`, `false`                    | `true`                          |
  | `late_arrivals_allowed` | `true`, `false`                    | `true`                          |

  `timer_seconds` is kept even when `auto_advance` is not `timer`: the chosen duration comes back if the timer is set again.

- **Only the room admin** changes the settings (`403` `room_admin_required` for another participant; [permissions](../product/permissions.md)).
- **During a game** (status `playing`), the settings are locked, **except late arrivals** (US-2.10): any other field answers `409` `room_settings_locked`.
- **Empty update**: a `PATCH` without any field (or with only `null` values) changes nothing.
- **Removing and banning** (US-2.11, US-2.12): the room admin removes a participant, at any time, a game included; the seat is deleted and the person can join again, unless banned. A banned account cannot join again; a banned guest is refused from the same browser only (§2.5).
- **Deletions**: purging a deleted template keeps the room, without template (`template: null`); no new game can then start in it. Deleting the room admin's account deletes their rooms and their participants.

## 2. Technical design

### 2.1 Options considered

| Topic         | Options                                                      | Choice and reason                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Settings      | Separate `room_settings` table (1-1); **columns of `rooms`** | **Columns**: the domain model makes them a 1-1 composition of the room; columns avoid a join and an orphan row. The API still groups them in a `settings` object.                                      |
| Game lock     | Wait for the games (#91); **`status` open / playing now**    | **Status now**: the "locked during a game" rule is built and tested with the settings; the game start (#91) only sets `playing`. "Closed" is not a status: a closed room is deleted (§2.6).            |
| Code          | Sequential; UUID; **short random code**                      | **Short random code**: typed or dictated by players, impossible to guess from other codes. A unique constraint and a new draw on collision keep it unique.                                             |
| Admin's seat  | Role only in `rooms.admin_id`; **participant row too**       | **Participant row**: the admin counts in the participant limit even when not playing (US-2.6), and their seat gives access like any other participant.                                                 |
| Addressing    | By UUID; **by code**                                         | **By code**: the [real-time guide](realtime.md) addresses the room channel and its REST snapshot by code (`/rooms/{code}`).                                                                            |
| Guest token   | Signed JWT; **opaque random token, hashed in the database**  | **Opaque token**: like a refresh token (256 bits, only the SHA-256 is stored), so a leak of the database gives no usable token. A seat dies with its room (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), which kills its link. |
| Personal link | Token in the query string; **token in the fragment**         | **Fragment** (`/r/<code>#guest=<token>`): a browser never sends it to the server, so it never reaches the access logs; the frontend reads it and removes it from the address once used.                |
| Validity      | Sliding; **fixed from the arrival**                          | **Fixed**: `GUEST_LINK_TTL_DAYS` (30 at most, as the product rules require), not extended by later visits.                                                                                             |

### 2.2 Data model

```
users ──< rooms ──< participants >── users
            │  └──< room_bans >── users
            └──> templates (SET NULL)
```

| Table          | Columns                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rooms`        | `id`, `code` (6, unique), `admin_id` → `users`, `template_id` → `templates` (nullable), `status`, `item_order`, `auto_advance`, `timer_seconds`, `cooldown_seconds`, `admin_plays`, `late_arrivals_allowed`, `version`, dates | `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards `users`, `ON DELETE SET NULL` towards `templates`; indexes on `admin_id` and `template_id`. `status`, `item_order`, `auto_advance`: text columns with a `CHECK` constraint (`ck_rooms_*`), like `images.status`.                                                                                                                                       |
| `participants` | `id`, `room_id` → `rooms`, `user_id` → `users` (nullable), `nickname` (50), `guest_token_hash` (64, unique), `guest_expires_at`, `connected`, dates                                                                           | `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards both. Unique `(room_id, user_id)`: one seat per user and room (invariant 10); it also serves as the index on `room_id`. Index on `user_id`. `ck_participants_account_or_guest`: either an account, or a guest with its nickname, token hash and expiry. Partial unique index `uq_participants_room_id_lower_nickname` on `(room_id, lower(nickname))`. |
| `room_bans`    | `id`, `room_id` → `rooms`, `user_id` → `users` (nullable), `guest_token_hash` (64, unique), `display_name` (50), dates                                                                                                        | `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards both. `ck_room_bans_account_or_guest`: either an account or a guest's token hash. Unique `(room_id, user_id)`, which also serves as the index on `room_id`; index on `user_id`.                                                                                                                                                                        |

#148 added `room_bans` (§2.5). Real time (#85) added `participants.connected` (presence, written only when it changes) and `rooms.version` (incremented with each change of the room); see the [real-time guide](realtime.md#3-in-the-code). The closed games no longer depend on the seats (`game_players`, [games guide](games.md)): closing a room deletes the seats with the room (§2.6).

### 2.3 Code generation

`generate_room_code()` (`app/services/rooms.py`) draws `ROOM_CODE_LENGTH` characters of `ROOM_CODE_ALPHABET` with `secrets.choice`. `RoomService.create` inserts the room in a **savepoint** (`session.begin_nested()`): when the insert breaks the unique constraint `uq_rooms_code`, only the savepoint is rolled back and a new code is drawn, up to `ROOM_CODE_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (5) times. With 32^6 (about a billion) codes, a single collision is already rare; reaching the limit is logged as an error and answers `500`. Any other integrity error is raised as is.

### 2.4 Guests

Decided and built in #87 (the _Guest links_ question of the [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md#technical-questions)). Someone without an account joins with a **nickname** (US-2.5) and comes back to their seat later (US-5.4). A guest is **never linked to an account**.

- **Nickname**: 1 to 50 characters, surrounding spaces removed (same rule as an account's display name). Unique in the room **without case**, against every displayed name, guests and accounts: otherwise `409` `nickname_taken`. The partial unique index backs the rule between guests; when it refuses a nickname the service let through (PostgreSQL `lower()` and Python `str.lower()` differ on a few Unicode characters), the answer is the same `409`, not a `500`. An **account** joins without any name check (US-2.4: no extra step), so it may share the name of a guest already there; the lobby tells them apart with the "guest" mention.
- **Token**: `generate_guest_token` (`secrets.token_urlsafe`, 256 bits). Only `hash_guest_token` (SHA-256) is stored, in `participants.guest_token_hash` (`app/core/security.py`).
- **Cookie** `guest_token`: `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Strict`, `Secure` with `AUTH_COOKIE_SECURE`, `Path` = `ROOM_COOKIE_PATH` + `/<code>` (e.g. `/api/rooms/K7P2QX`, the path seen by the browser through the proxy), `Max-Age` = the remaining validity. One cookie per room: the browser only sends it to that room's routes. The browser is recognised by this cookie, **never by its IP address**.
- **Personal link**: `FRONTEND_BASE_URL/r/<code>#guest=<token>`, returned **once** by `POST /guests` (`guest_link`, `guest_link_expires_at`). `POST /guest-session {token}` gives the seat back on any device and sets the cookie there; the validity is not extended. The link is shown only once, since only its hash is stored: a guest who lost it, still recognised by the cookie, asks for a **new link** (`POST /guest-link`). A new token replaces the old one, whose link and cookie stop working; the expiry date does not change.
- **Validity**: `GUEST_LINK_TTL_DAYS` from the arrival. An expired seat is lost ("Left"): it disappears from the participants, its cookie and link answer `404`, and its nickname becomes free again (the next guest join removes the expired seats of the room first). Closing a room (§2.6) deletes its seats, hence their links.
- **Reading the room**: `GET /rooms/{code}` takes the account first (Bearer, checked as everywhere: an invalid token answers `401` so that the client refreshes its session), otherwise the guest cookie of that room. Without either, `404` `room_not_found`, like a user without a seat. A guest is never room admin; `PATCH /settings` and `POST /join` stay reserved to accounts (`401`).
- The real-time channel (#85) authenticates guests with the same cookie ([real-time guide](realtime.md)).

### 2.5 Removal and bans

Built in #148 (US-2.11, US-2.12).

- **Removal**: `DELETE /rooms/{code}/participants/{participant_id}` (room admin only, `403` `room_admin_required` otherwise) deletes the seat, under the room lock, at any time, a game included: their placements are deleted with the seat (`ON DELETE CASCADE`, [games guide](games.md#rules)). An unknown participant, or a guest whose link expired, answers `404` `participant_not_found`; the room admin's own seat, `409` `cannot_remove_room_admin`. The version is incremented; after the commit the service asks the channel to close the person's sockets (`4410` `removed_from_room`), **then** publishes `participant.removed` to the room: the registry drops their sockets first, so they do not receive it. A removed guest's personal link and cookie stop working, since the seat holding their hash is gone. Without a ban, the person can join again with the link or the code.
- **Ban**: `?ban=true` also writes a `room_bans` row, and the sockets close with `4411` `room_banned`. For an **account**, the row holds `user_id`: `POST /join` answers `403` `room_banned`, whatever the case of the code. For a **guest**, it holds the `guest_token_hash` of the seat: the browser still has its `guest_token` cookie (30 days, path of the room), which goes with `POST /guests`, so that browser is refused with the same `403`. This is **best effort**: a private window or cleared cookies get around it, since the app never uses the IP address; the room admin is warned in the dialog, and locking arrivals during a game stops them for real.
- **Rejected option**: drawing a new room code at each guest ban. It would also lose every other guest's seat, since their cookies are stored under the old code's path, and it breaks the shared link.
- **Unban**: `DELETE /rooms/{code}/bans/{ban_id}` (room admin only; `404` `ban_not_found`). Only the room admin sees the bans (`bans` in `RoomResponse`, empty for anyone else), so the room version does not change and nothing is published.
- **Display name**: the ban keeps the name at the time of the ban, the only name a guest ever had.

### 2.6 Closing a room

Built in #89 (US-2.7), automatic closing in #90 (US-2.8).

- **Route**: `DELETE /rooms/{code}` (room admin only, `403` `room_admin_required` otherwise; `404` `room_not_found`) answers `204`. `GameService.close_room` does it under the room lock, in one transaction.
- **Game in progress**: it is closed first (`_close_game`, [games guide](games.md#26-final-cooldown)): its players are frozen, so its placements no longer depend on the seats about to be deleted. During a round, the current round ends and the current rankings become the final boards (`_start_final_boards`), so the game keeps results; the interrupted item gives no absent votes. During the cooldown, the final boards are kept as they are. The frontend asks for confirmation in both cases, and says when a game will be closed first.
- **Deletion**: the room row is deleted. The database deletes its seats and bans (`ON DELETE CASCADE`) and detaches its games (`games.room_id` set to `NULL`): the games survive their room (invariant 11). The link and the code stop working (`404` `room_not_found`), and so do the guests' personal links and cookies (`404` `guest_link_invalid`).
- **Sockets**: after the commit, the service asks the channel to close every socket of the room with `4412` `room_closed` (`RoomEventPublisher.close_room`); nothing is published, the room no longer exists. The page says "The room admin closed this room." with a link home; the room admin who closed it goes home.
- **Timers**: a timer still scheduled for the game finds no room any more (`_locked_room_of_game`) and does nothing.
- **Account deletion**: deleting the account of a room admin closes each of their rooms the same way, once the deletion is confirmed and before the account is deleted (`AuthService.delete_account(before_delete=...)` with `GameService.close_rooms_of_admin`, one transaction per room). Without it, the database would delete the rooms through `rooms.admin_id`, with the placements of a game in progress, and nobody would be told.
- **Automatic closing** (#90, US-2.8): an open room where nobody is connected, and that has not changed for `ROOM_INACTIVITY_HOURS` (24 h by default), is closed the same way. "Changed" is read from `rooms.updated_at`, which moves with each `version` (a join, a departure or a disconnection, the settings, a game), so the delay starts when the last participant leaves; the heartbeat writes nothing, so a participant connected without doing anything keeps the room open. A room in game is never closed for inactivity (lifecycles: Playing → Closed only by the room admin).
- **Sweep**: `room_sweeper` (`app/services/room_sweeper.py`), started by the lifespan of `app/main.py`, runs `GameService.close_inactive_rooms` every `ROOM_INACTIVITY_SWEEP_MINUTES` (15 by default) in the threadpool, with a short session. It runs in the process that holds the sockets, since it closes them (`4412`); a cron script could not. `list_inactive_room_ids` reads the candidates, then each room is checked again under its lock (someone may have come back) and closed in its own transaction. The first sweep comes one interval after startup, so the participants connected before a restart can come back first. A failed sweep is logged and the next one tries again.

### 2.7 Settings

| Variable                        | Purpose                                                                                                                 | Default      |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| `ROOM_DEFAULT_TIMER_SECONDS`    | Timer per item of a new room, in seconds (5 to 300)                                                                     | `30`         |
| `ROOM_DEFAULT_COOLDOWN_SECONDS` | Final cooldown of a new room, in seconds (10 to 600)                                                                    | `60`         |
| `GUEST_LINK_TTL_DAYS`           | Validity of a guest's personal link and cookie, in days from the arrival (30 at most)                                   | `30`         |
| `ROOM_COOKIE_PATH`              | Path of the room routes as seen by the browser, base of the guest cookie (`/rooms` in the API tests, without the proxy) | `/api/rooms` |
| `ROOM_INACTIVITY_HOURS`         | An open room where nobody is connected and without change for this many hours is closed (§2.6)                          | `24`         |
| `ROOM_INACTIVITY_SWEEP_MINUTES` | Interval of the sweep that closes the inactive rooms, in minutes                                                        | `15`         |

The bounds are constants (`app/constants/rooms.py`): the API validates the durations with them and returns them as `ge` / `le` params, which the frontend displays without copying them. The defaults are validated against the same bounds when the application starts.

### 2.8 Endpoints

Every route requires the Bearer access token (`401` otherwise), except the guest routes and `GET /rooms/{code}` (§2.4).

| Method and path                                      | Body                                                                                                    | Success                            | Errors                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `POST /rooms`                                        | `{template_id}`                                                                                         | `201` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `404`, `409`, `422`        |
| `GET /rooms/{code}`                                  | —                                                                                                       | `200` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `404`                      |
| `POST /rooms/{code}/join`                            | —                                                                                                       | `200` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `403`, `404`, `409`        |
| `POST /rooms/{code}/guests`                          | `{nickname}`                                                                                            | `201` `GuestRoomResponse` + cookie | `403`, `404`, `409`, `422`        |
| `POST /rooms/{code}/guest-session`                   | `{token}`                                                                                               | `200` `GuestRoomResponse` + cookie | `404`, `422`                      |
| `POST /rooms/{code}/guest-link`                      | —                                                                                                       | `200` `GuestRoomResponse` + cookie | `404`                             |
| `PATCH /rooms/{code}/settings`                       | `{item_order?, auto_advance?, timer_seconds?, cooldown_seconds?, admin_plays?, late_arrivals_allowed?}` | `200` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `403`, `404`, `409`, `422` |
| `POST /rooms/{code}/game`                            | —                                                                                                       | `201` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `403`, `404`, `409`        |
| `DELETE /rooms/{code}/participants/{participant_id}` | query `ban` (`false` by default)                                                                        | `200` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `403`, `404`, `409`, `422` |
| `DELETE /rooms/{code}/bans/{ban_id}`                 | —                                                                                                       | `200` `RoomResponse`               | `401`, `403`, `404`, `422`        |
| `DELETE /rooms/{code}`                               | —                                                                                                       | `204`                              | `401`, `403`, `404`               |

- `RoomResponse` is `{code, version, link, status, template: {id, name} | null, settings: {item_order, auto_advance, timer_seconds, cooldown_seconds, admin_plays, late_arrivals_allowed}, participants: [{id, display_name, is_admin, is_guest, connected}], is_admin, bans: [{id, display_name, is_guest}], game: {round_number, round_count, current_item: {id, text, image_url}, ends_at} | null, past_games: [{public_token, closed_at, player_count}]}` (the game in progress, [games guide](games.md)); `display_name` is the account's name or the guest's nickname, and expired guest seats are left out. `GuestRoomResponse` adds `guest_link` and `guest_link_expires_at`. `GET /rooms/{code}` accepts an account or a guest (§2.4); the other routes need the Bearer access token. `participants` are in order of arrival, the room admin first; `is_admin` says whether the user making the request is the room admin; `bans` is filled for the room admin only; `past_games` lists the closed games of the room, newest first, with their results link ([results guide](results.md#26-results-api)); `player_count` is computed by the query (`Game.player_count`, a `query_expression`), without loading the players.
- `PATCH /rooms/{code}/settings`, closing, and the removal and unban routes lock the room row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) until their commit, as the [real-time guide](realtime.md) requires for every change of a room.

### 2.9 Known limitations

- Games: the start (#91) sets `playing` and freezes the template ([games guide](games.md)); the rounds, the placements and coming back during a game are described there.
- A room in game where everyone left, with the "everyone placed" setting, waits for the room admin: nobody connected never ends the round, and a room in game is never closed for inactivity.

Live updates: once the room is loaded, `useRoomEvents` follows its real-time channel ([real-time guide](realtime.md#3-in-the-code)): arrivals and settings read the room again, the presence and the removals update the cached room, and the lobby shows a presence dot per participant.

**Signed out**: the room page opens without an account (outside `RequireAuth`). A returning guest is recognised by the cookie; a newcomer sees `JoinRoomScreen`: "Sign in" sends to `/login` and back to the room after signing in, or after signing up (`RegisterPage` and the links between both pages keep the requested page), or they join as a guest. Google sign-in still comes back to the home page.
