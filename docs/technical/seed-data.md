# Seed data

English | [Français](seed-data.fr.md)

A realistic set of test data — accounts, templates, images, closed games, open rooms, moderation log — to try the application for real, in local development and on staging. Never in production.

## 1. What is created

| Data           | Quantity and cases                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accounts       | 36: 6 super admins (`seed-admin-1` to `-6`), 24 confirmed players (`seed-player-1` to `-24`), 3 unconfirmed addresses (`seed-unverified-…`), 3 Google accounts without password (`seed-google-…`). 2 players banned by the moderation, the last ones: `seed-player-23` until 14 days from the seed run, `seed-player-24` permanently; they cannot sign in (§3) |
| Templates      | 30: 3 empty, 9 text (6 to 10 tiles), 6 with images (12 tiles), 6 full (32 tiles, the maximum), 6 deleted. 2 hidden by the moderation, first the templates of the rooms left open: the editor shows the reason, the lobby has no "Start the game" button                                                                                                        |
| Images         | 72, generated (a colour, a shape, a number), uploaded to the real image storage. 3 hidden and 1 deleted by the moderation: the "image removed" visual in templates, closed games and results. Their upload dates are spread over the last 30 days, for the filters of the recent uploads                                                                       |
| Closed games   | 75 over 90 days, 1 to 9 players with guests, absent votes and items nobody placed. `seed-player-1` is in about 60 of them: a history of 3 or 4 pages                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Open rooms     | 9: 3 whose lobby shows their past games, 3 full (10 seats), 3 with a banned account and a banned guest, with varied settings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Moderation log | 180 entries of the 6 super admins over 120 days, every action and target, plus the 4 actions on images, the 2 on templates, the 2 bans above and a ban followed by its lift, which target the real image, template or account: 4 pages                                                                                                                         |

- Every address is `seed-…@seed.example.com`: a reserved domain, no email can arrive there.
- Every account has the same password: `seed-password-local` locally, the `SEED_PASSWORD` secret on staging.
- The data is the same at each run: the random choices use a fixed seed.

## 2. Running it

**Locally**, with the database and the image storage of `compose.yaml` started (about 10 seconds):

```bash
cd backend
uv run python scripts/seed.py
```

**On staging**: GitHub → Actions → **Seed staging** → Run workflow (`seed-staging.yml`). The staging pod has no shell, so the script runs on a GitHub runner, with the database and image storage settings of the `staging` environment. It takes about 5 minutes: each query crosses the Internet (§4). Before the first run, add the `SEED_PASSWORD` secret to that environment. If the database address seen from a runner differs from the app pod's one, set the `SEED_POSTGRES_HOST` and `SEED_POSTGRES_PORT` variables.

The script logs each of its 6 steps with its duration, and its progress inside the long ones (each template, each image, each closed game, every 30 log entries), so that the workflow log shows where it is. It ends with what it removed and created, and how long it took.

## 3. Rules

- **It replaces its own data, and only it.** Each run first deletes everything owned by the `seed-…@seed.example.com` accounts, then creates a new set. Other accounts are never touched: on staging, real test accounts stay. Games of a seed room that a real account played are deleted with the seed data.
- **Never in production.** The script refuses any database that is not `tierlist` (local) or `…_staging`, and the workflow is bound to the `staging` environment only.
- **No known password on a public site.** On a `…_staging` database, `SEED_PASSWORD` is required: the local password is written in the repository and would give anyone a super admin account.
- **Open rooms do not last.** A room nobody is connected to is closed after `ROOM_INACTIVITY_HOURS` (24 h). Run the seed again to get the rooms back; the closed games stay.
- **Deleted templates** are deleted at seed time: the nightly purge erases them 30 days later.
- **Banned accounts cannot sign in**: the sign-in shows the reason of their ban. `seed-player-1`, used by the tests and the documentation, is never banned. One more player had a ban lifted: they sign in, and their account page shows a past ban. The temporary ban ends 14 days after the run: run the seed again to get it back.

## 4. How it is built

`backend/app/seed/`, one module per domain, chained by `run.py`:

| Module          | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sizes.py`      | `FULL_SIZE` (the set above) and `SMALL_SIZE` (the same cases, fewer of each, for the tests).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `guard.py`      | The database check and the `SEED_PASSWORD` rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `cleanup.py`    | Deletes the seed data in an order that leaves no orphan row (log, games, snapshots, image rows and files, then accounts), because several links to the accounts are `SET NULL`. The file of a hidden image is erased under `quarantine/`; a deleted image has no file left. The bans of the seed accounts go with them (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `accounts.py`   | `AuthService.register`, then the role and the confirmed address; Google accounts like a first Google sign-in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `templates.py`  | `TemplateService.create` (the template and its default tiers), then its tiles written in one go; images through `ImageService.upload` (one image owner per template with images: at most 120 uploads per hour and per account), returned in `SeedTemplates.images` for the moderation. The upload dates are then spread over the last `UPLOAD_SPAN_DAYS` (30) days with the seed's random generator (`_spread_upload_dates`, one `UPDATE` grouped by primary key), so the recent uploads have dates to filter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `seats.py`      | The seats (accounts, guests) and bans of the seed rooms, written in one go.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `games.py`      | Closed games and their snapshots, written directly (below) by batches of 10, and the open rooms that keep their games.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `rooms.py`      | Full rooms and rooms with bans: created by `RoomService` (code, room admin's seat, settings; with a publisher that sends nothing, nobody is connected).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `moderation.py` | Log entries written with their date in one go, as `ModerationLogService.record` writes them (actor and target names copied). `moderate_images`: hides and deletes random images of the templates with images (`hidden_images`, `deleted_images` in `sizes.py`) through the real `ImageModerationService`, so their files go to the quarantine or are erased, and their log entries target the real image. `moderate_templates`: hides `hidden_templates` templates (`sizes.py`) through the real `TemplateModerationService`, with a random reason; `run.py` picks them (`_templates_to_hide`): first the templates of the rooms left open whose lobby shows their past games, then the other playable templates, without duplicates. `ban_accounts`: bans accounts through the real `AccountBanService` (signed out, log entry targeting the account), the first ones until `TEMPORARY_BAN_DAYS` (14) days from now, the next ones permanently, with a random reason, and bans then lifts the last ones (`accounts_with_a_lifted_ban`); `run.py` takes them among the last players (`temporarily_banned_accounts`, `permanently_banned_accounts` in `sizes.py`). |

**Why direct writes.** On staging every query crosses the Internet, from the GitHub runner to the database (about 0.2 s). Going through the services everywhere took 7,500 queries (24 minutes): `TemplateService.add_tile` reloads and commits the template for each tile, `RoomService.join` reloads the whole room for each arrival, `take_snapshot` reloads the template for each game, and playing a game through `GameService`, one placement at a time, reloads the room at every call. So:

- **Through the services**: accounts (`AuthService.register`), templates and their default tiers (`TemplateService.create`), image uploads (`ImageService.upload`), image moderation (`ImageModerationService`), template moderation (`TemplateModerationService`), account bans (`AccountBanService`), rooms (`RoomService.create`, `update_settings`), template deletion.
- **Written directly, following the rules of the service they replace**:
  - tiles: a text or an image each, at most 32, continuous positions, images of the owner;
  - snapshots: a copy of the name, the tiers and the tiles, with the same images;
  - seats and bans: an account at most once per room, unique guest nicknames whatever the case, at most 10 seats, a guest token kept as its hash only, a ban that replaces the seat;
  - closed games: the state `GameService._close_game` leaves (one round per tile, one frozen player per person who plays, a ROUND placement per item, without tier for an absent vote, a FINAL placement at the same place for each placed item, continuous positions in each tier, a results token);
  - log entries (except those of the images, templates and bans): the actor and target names copied.
- The accounts are read once: after each commit, SQLAlchemy forgets the loaded rows and would read each account again.

A full run now makes about 1,400 queries (about 5 minutes on staging). `test_seed.py` checks that the small set stays under 500 queries, so that a return to one-by-one writes does not go unnoticed.

### Keeping it up to date

When a feature adds data or cases worth trying for real (reports, public templates, likes…), the seed covers them in the same stack ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)): a new module or new cases in an existing one, a quantity in `sizes.py`, the deletion in `cleanup.py` if a new link to the accounts is `SET NULL`, and the tests (`test_seed.py`).

Tests: [testing guide](testing.md) (`test_seed.py`, `test_seed_guard.py`).
