# Games

English | [Français](games.fr.md)

How a game is played in a room: its start, its rounds, its placements, the move to the next round, its final cooldown and its closing. **Built in #91** for the start, **#92** for the placements and **#93** for the round progression (next, timer, everyone placed, absent votes), **#94** for the game screen, **#95** for coming back to one's seat and joining during a game and **#96** for the final cooldown (final placements, catch-up, end); **#97** for its screen and **#98** for saving the closed game; its results (#99) and the history (#103) come next.

## 1. Functional overview

The room admin starts a game from the lobby (US-3.1). The game is frozen on the current version of the template, the item order is fixed, and the first item is shown to everyone. Each participant who places drops the current item in a tier, at the position they want (US-3.3), on a **private board**: the others only see who has placed it.

| Action                 | Endpoint                                      | Result                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Start a game           | `POST /rooms/{code}/game`                     | The room in `playing`, with its game: round 1 of N, the current item (text and/or image) and the end of its timer when there is one.                                       |
| Place the current item | `PUT /rooms/{code}/game/placements/{tile_id}` | Body `{tier_id, position}`. The room, with the reader's board updated (`own_placements`) and who has placed the current item. Placing it again in the same round moves it. |
| Next item              | `POST /rooms/{code}/game/next`                | Room admin only. Ends the round in progress and shows the next item to everyone, or starts the final cooldown after the last item.                                         |
| End the cooldown       | `POST /rooms/{code}/game/end`                 | Room admin only, during the cooldown. Closes the game: the room comes back `open`, without a game.                                                                         |

### Rules

- **Who**: the room admin only (`403` `room_admin_required`); a reader without a seat gets `404` `room_not_found`, like every room route.
- **One game at a time** (invariant 3): a room already `playing` answers `409` `game_in_progress`. A partial unique index on `games(room_id)` (`status <> 'closed'`) is the last safety net.
- **Someone must place the items** (US-3.1, US-3.5): the placing participants are the seated participants, minus the room admin when they do not play (`admin_plays` off); they keep only the controls. Without any, `409` `no_placing_participant` (the room admin alone, not playing). Being connected is not required: a participant who is not there gets absent votes, like in any round (#93).
- **Template**: the template of the room must still exist and have tiles. A purged template answers `409` `template_not_found`, a template without tiles `409` `template_has_no_tiles`.
- **Frozen version** (invariant 4): `take_snapshot` ([templates guide](templates.md#24-snapshots-template-versions)) copies the tiers and tiles; editing the template afterwards never changes the game.
- **Item order** (invariant 5): the snapshot tiles in template order, or shuffled when `item_order` is `random`. One round per tile, numbered from 1.
- **First round**: started at once; with `auto_advance = timer`, its end (`ends_at`) is now + `timer_seconds`.
- **Settings kept with the game**: `item_order` and `admin_plays`, so that they stay known once the room is closed (the results exclude the room admin who did not play).
- **Who places** (US-3.3, US-3.5): every placing participant, account or guest (the guest with the cookie of the room); the room admin who does not play gets `403` `not_a_placing_participant`.
- **Only the current item** (US-3.3): the tile must be the item of the current round, otherwise `409` `item_not_current` (an item of a past round cannot move before the cooldown, a future one is not shown yet). Without a game, `409` `round_not_in_progress`; during the cooldown, see below. The tier must be one of the game's frozen version, otherwise `404` `tier_not_found`.
- **Tier and position**: positions start at `0` and stay **continuous** in each tier, per participant: placing at `k` shifts the next items, a position past the end puts the item last, and the tier it leaves closes up. The relative order of past items never changes. Placing the item again in the same round replaces its placement (invariant 6: one round placement per tile).
- **Private board**: the response only holds the reader's own placements; the others' are never sent, only **who** has placed the current item (`placed_participant_ids`).
- **Removed participant**: during a game, their placements are deleted with their seat; they are no longer in the game nor in its results. Once the game is closed, its placements no longer depend on the seats (§2.6).
- **Next item** (US-3.4): the room admin ends the round at any time, whatever the automatic setting and even if some have not placed; a player gets `403` `room_admin_required`, and outside a round (no game, cooldown) `409` `round_not_in_progress`.
- **Timer** (`auto_advance = timer`): each round ends at its `ends_at` (start + `timer_seconds`), decided by the server alone. Once `ends_at` is passed, even before the timer fires, a placement gets `409` `round_not_in_progress`: a late placement cannot end the round any more.
- **Everyone placed** (`auto_advance = everyone_placed`): the round ends when every **connected** placing participant has placed the current item. A disconnected participant is not waited for; at least one placing participant must be connected (nobody connected never ends the round). It is checked after a first placement, every presence change (a departure, but also a return: a returning player who has placed unblocks a round nobody was waiting for) and a removal.
- **Absent votes** (US-4.3): when a round ends, every placing participant without a round placement for the item gets an absent vote (no tier, no position), connected or not.
- **Late arrival** (US-4.5): a participant who joins during a game gets an absent vote for every round already over, and places from the current round.
- **Coming back** (US-4.4): a participant who loses the connection keeps their seat and their board; the rounds that end meanwhile give them absent votes. They come back through `POST /join` (account), the room cookie (guest, same browser) or the personal link (guest, any device), even when arrivals are locked or the room is full. The room they get holds their board (`own_placements`) and the current round, and the real-time channel reconnects then reads the room again ([real-time guide](realtime.md)).
- **Last round**: when it ends, the game moves to `cooldown`; its end (`cooldown_ends_at`) is now + `cooldown_seconds`.
- **Final cooldown** (US-4.1, US-4.2): every placing participant can move **any** of their tiles. Their final board starts as a copy of their round board; a move changes the final placement only, the round placement is kept.
- **Catching up** (US-4.6): the items with an absent vote (or all of them for a late arrival) are listed apart (`missed_items`) and can be placed: they get a final placement and keep their absent vote. A tile outside the game gets `404` `item_not_in_game`; once `cooldown_ends_at` is passed, `409` `cooldown_over`.
- **End of the cooldown** (US-4.1): at `cooldown_ends_at` (server timer) or when the room admin ends it, the game is `closed` and the room `open` again; a player gets `403` `room_admin_required`, and outside the cooldown `409` `cooldown_not_in_progress`. The closed game is saved (§2.6): its rankings no longer change, and they outlive the seats and the room.

## 2. Technical design

### 2.1 Data model

```
rooms ──< games (room_id SET NULL) ──< rounds >── snapshot_tiles
             ├──> template_snapshots
             ├──< placements >── participants (CASCADE), game_players (CASCADE), snapshot_tiles, snapshot_tiers
             └──< game_players >── users (SET NULL)
```

| Table          | Columns                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `games`        | `id`, `room_id` → `rooms` (nullable), `snapshot_id` → `template_snapshots`, `admin_id` → `users` (nullable, `SET NULL`: the room admin at the start, for the history), `status`, `item_order`, `admin_plays`, `current_round_number`, `started_at`, `cooldown_ends_at`, `closed_at`, dates | `ON DELETE SET NULL` towards `rooms`: a game **outlives** its room (invariant 11). `status`: `in_round`, `cooldown`, `closed` (text column with a `CHECK`, like the room). Partial unique index `uq_games_room_id_in_progress`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `rounds`       | `id`, `game_id` → `games`, `number`, `snapshot_tile_id` → `snapshot_tiles`, `started_at`, `ends_at`, `ended_at`                                                                                                                                                                            | `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards `games`. Unique `(game_id, number)` and `(game_id, snapshot_tile_id)`: one round per tile. A round points at a **snapshot** tile, never at a template tile.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `placements`   | `id`, `game_id` → `games`, `participant_id` → `participants` (nullable), `player_id` → `game_players` (nullable), `snapshot_tile_id` → `snapshot_tiles`, `phase`, `snapshot_tier_id` → `snapshot_tiers` (nullable), `position` (nullable), dates                                           | `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards `games`, `participants` and `game_players`. A seat during the game, a frozen player once it is closed: `ck_placements_seat_or_player` (exactly one of them). `phase`: `round` or `final` (#96). Unique `(game_id, participant_id, snapshot_tile_id, phase)` and `(game_id, player_id, snapshot_tile_id, phase)` (invariant 6). `ck_placements_tier_and_position`: a tier and a position `>= 0`, or neither for an **absent** vote (invariant 8, #93); `ck_placements_final_not_absent`. No unique constraint on the position: shifting the next items would break it midway, the service keeps them continuous. |
| `game_players` | `id`, `game_id` → `games`, `user_id` → `users` (nullable), `display_name`, dates                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The players of a closed game, frozen at closing time (US-5.1). `ON DELETE CASCADE` towards `games`, `SET NULL` towards `users`: deleting the account keeps the player in the game. Unique `(game_id, user_id)`; index on `user_id` for the history (#103).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

All the rounds are created at the start; only round 1 has `started_at`. `ends_at` is the timer end (server time, UTC), `ended_at` the actual end (#93).

### 2.2 Start

`GameService.start` (`app/services/games.py`) runs in **one transaction**:

1. Locks the room and checks the room admin (`RoomService.get_for_admin_change`), then the status, the template and the placing participants.
2. `TemplateService.take_snapshot` locks the template and only flushes.
3. Orders the snapshot tiles (`shuffle_items`, `random.SystemRandom`, for `random`), creates the game and its rounds, starts round 1.
4. Sets the room `playing`, increments `rooms.version`, commits.
5. **After the commit**, publishes `game.started` (`{round_number, round_count, ends_at}`) to the room ([real-time guide](realtime.md)).

The timer end is acted on by the server-side timers, and the pending deadlines are scheduled again at startup (§2.5, [real-time guide](realtime.md#28-server-side-timers)).

**Play again** (US-2.9, #102): once a game is closed, the room is `open` again and the room admin starts the next one through the same route, in the same room (same link and code). Nothing is carried over: `start` takes a **new snapshot** of the current template (the closed game keeps its own, invariant 4), and the players are the seats in the room at that moment (those who left are not, those who arrived are). The lobby button then says "Play again"; the results opened from the lobby offer it too ([results guide](results.md#27-results-page)).

### 2.3 What the room returns

`RoomResponse.game` is the game in progress **seen by the reader**, `null` outside a game:

```json
{
  "round_number": 1,
  "round_count": 12,
  "current_item": {
    "id": "…",
    "text": "Paprika",
    "image_url": "https://…/x.webp"
  },
  "ends_at": null,
  "tiers": [{ "id": "…", "name": "S", "color": "#FF3B6B" }],
  "placed_participant_ids": ["…"],
  "own_placements": [
    {
      "item": { "id": "…", "text": "Chorizo", "image_url": null },
      "tier_id": "…",
      "position": 0
    }
  ],
  "status": "in_round",
  "cooldown_ends_at": null,
  "missed_items": []
}
```

The current item and the frozen tiers (top to bottom) are shown to every participant. `placed_participant_ids` says **who** has placed the current item, never where. `own_placements` holds the reader's placements only, sorted by tier then position: their round placements during the rounds, their final placements during the cooldown. A participant who comes back finds their board; it is empty for the room admin who does not play. During the cooldown, `missed_items` lists the items the reader has not placed yet (absent votes, late arrival), in round order; it is empty during the rounds. The response is built for the reader's **seat** (`room_response(room, seat, …)`): the routes find it with `RoomService.seat_of` (account or guest cookie) or `seat_in` (an account in a room already read). The room routes take the image storage (`get_image_storage`) to build the public image URLs (`optional_image_url`).

### 2.4 Placements

`GameService.place` (`app/services/games.py`) runs in **one transaction**:

1. Finds the reader's seat and **locks the room** (`RoomService.seat_of(viewer, code, for_update=True)`): two placements in the same room run one after the other.
2. Checks the round in progress, that the seat places (`placing_participants`), the current item and the tier.
3. Works on the reader's board for the round phase: takes the placement of the tile out of its tier if it exists, inserts it at the position (bounded to the end of the tier) and renumbers both tiers `0..n-1` (`_place_in_tier`).
4. **First placement of the round**: increments `rooms.version`, commits, then publishes `participant.placed` (`{participant_id, round_number}`, no tier nor position). **A move** commits without a new version nor event: the others see nothing change.

Absent votes are created when a round ends (§2.5); final placements are built during the cooldown (§2.6).

### 2.5 Round progression

Three triggers end a round, and all of them run the same transition, `GameService._end_round`, **under the room lock**:

| Trigger         | Entry point                                                                                                                                     | Checked state                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Next item"     | `POST /rooms/{code}/game/next` → `next_round` (room admin, `get_for_admin_change`)                                                              | A round in progress, otherwise `409`.                                                            |
| Timer           | `RoundTimers` → `end_round_on_timer(game_id, round_number)`                                                                                     | The same round still in progress, otherwise nothing.                                             |
| Everyone placed | `place` (same transaction), `advance_if_everyone_placed(room_id)` after a departure (`routes/room_channel.py`) or a removal (`routes/rooms.py`) | `_everyone_placed`: the setting, at least one connected placing participant, all of them placed. |

`_end_round` sets `ended_at`, adds the absent votes, then either starts the next round (`started_at`, and `ends_at` with a timer) or moves the game to `cooldown` with `cooldown_ends_at`; it increments `rooms.version`. After the commit, the service publishes `round.started` (`{round_number, round_count, ends_at}`) or `cooldown.started` (`{ends_at}`), and schedules the next deadline (timer of the new round, or end of the cooldown) from the values of the event.

**Races**: the timer, "next" and the last placement may arrive together. Each one locks the room and checks the expected state first; the second one finds the round over and does nothing. Transitions are therefore **idempotent**, and a round ended early does not need its timer cancelled. The deadlines are scheduled after the commits, in any order: `RoundTimers` keeps a rank per game (the round number, then the cooldown) and ignores a deadline older than the one it holds. A failed transition (database unreachable for a moment) is tried again after 1, 5 then 30 seconds (`TIMER_RETRY_DELAYS_SECONDS`, `constants/games.py`).

**Server-side timers** (`services/round_timers.py`, [real-time guide](realtime.md#28-server-side-timers)): `RoundTimers` keeps one `asyncio` task per game, which waits for the `ends_at` of its current round and then calls `end_round_on_timer` through the threadpool, in a short session. `schedule` is thread-safe (`call_soon_threadsafe`, called from the REST routes after the commit); a new deadline for the same game replaces the previous one; a deadline already passed fires at once. At startup (`lifespan` in `main.py`), `pending_round_deadlines` reads the rounds in progress that wait for their timer and schedules them again: a restart loses no timer. The services receive it through the `RoundScheduler` protocol (`get_round_scheduler`), replaced by a recording fake in the tests.

**Late arrival**: `RoomService.join` and `join_as_guest` call `_absent_for_past_rounds` in the same transaction as the new seat.

### 2.6 Final cooldown

**Final board**: when the last round ends, `_end_round` also calls `_start_final_boards`: every round placement (absent votes aside) is copied into a `final` placement, same tier, same position. An item nobody moves keeps a final placement equal to its round placement (US-4.2) without any computation at read time; a move changes the copy only.

**Placing during the cooldown**: `place` sends a game in `cooldown` to `_place_final`, on the reader's `final` board, with the same `_place_in_tier`. Any tile of the game can be placed (`item_not_in_game` otherwise). A tile without a final placement (absent vote, late arrival) gets a new one and keeps its absent round vote (US-4.6). The board is private: no version, no event. Once `cooldown_ends_at` is passed, even before the timer fires, `409` `round_not_in_progress`.

**End of the cooldown**: `_close_game` sets the game to `closed` with `closed_at`, sets the room back to `open` **in the same transaction** and increments `rooms.version`; after the commit, `game.closed` (`{game_id}`) is published. `Room.current_game` skips closed games, so the room comes back without a game. Two triggers run it under the room lock:

| Trigger   | Entry point                                                                         | Checked state                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| "End now" | `POST /rooms/{code}/game/end` → `end_cooldown` (room admin, `get_for_admin_change`) | A cooldown in progress, otherwise `409`.            |
| Timer     | `RoundTimers` → `end_cooldown_on_timer(game_id)`                                    | The same game still in cooldown, otherwise nothing. |

`RoundTimers` keeps a single deadline per game: `schedule_cooldown_end` replaces the deadline of the last round. A late round timer calls `end_round_on_timer`, which does nothing in cooldown, so it can never close the game. At startup, `pending_cooldown_ends` schedules the cooldowns in progress again.

**Saving the closed game** (US-5.1): `_close_game` also calls `_freeze_players`. Each seat with placements in the game (players and late arrivals, never the room admin who does not play) becomes a `GamePlayer` (`game_players`: the game, the account, the name at closing time), and all its placements move from `participant_id` to `player_id`. Closing the room ([rooms guide](rooms.md#26-closing-a-room)), removing the seat or deleting the account (`user_id` set to `NULL`) no longer touches the closed game. The game in progress keeps reading `participant_id`: the frozen rows of past games are never among its placements. `_close_game` also draws the game's results link (`public_token`, [results guide](results.md#26-results-api)). The results read `game_players`, and so will the history (#103).

### 2.7 Reading the room

The room is loaded with everything its answer shows (`_room_with_details`) in a constant number of queries, whatever the number of items (`test_game_queries.py`):

- **Seats keep their room.** The loaded room is attached to each of its seats (`set_committed_value`). The session's identity map only keeps weak references: a caller that kept the seat alone (`seat_of`) lost the room, and `seat.room` reloaded it lazily with its game, one query per tile.
- **Mutations read the room again after their commit.** The commit expires the session, so `start`, `place`, `next_round`, `end_cooldown` and `remove_participant` read the room again in one go (`_reloaded`, `_reloaded_seat`) instead of letting the answer reload it object by object.
- **No board for the room admin who does not play.** They get neither `own_placements` nor `missed_items`.

The frontend ignores an answer older than the room it already has (`storeRoom`): a smaller version, or at equal version a game behind (round, status), since a move does not change the version.

## 4. Known limitations

- Drag and drop with a pointer is not covered by the automated tests (jsdom has no layout): they place with the buttons, and `boardPositions.test.ts` covers the position computations.
- "Who places" is computed by the backend (`placing_participants`) and again by the screen (`GameScreen`, from `admin_plays` and `is_admin`) to show the board and the counter.
- The history of the accounts comes with #103.
- Closing a room during a game closes the game first ([rooms guide](rooms.md#26-closing-a-room)); during a round, the current rankings become the final boards. Games left without a room by an older version (before #89) are skipped at startup.
- "A game is in progress" lives in two places: `rooms.status = playing` and a `games` row not `closed`. The start writes both in one transaction, under the room lock; the end of the cooldown sets the room back to `open` in the same transaction as the game.
